BAGA DHUFTAN

Welcome to Khoobaa Dubbii

Baga nagaan dhuftan! Welcome to Khoobaa Dubbii. I am thrilled to have you here. This site is a dedicated sanctuary for the timeless mammaaksa (proverbs) of the Oromoo people.

In our fast-paced world, it is easy to forget the profound wisdom passed down by our ancestors. These proverbs are more than just old sayings; they are a guide to life, a reflection of our culture, and a bridge connecting our past to our future.

My mission is simple: to collect, preserve, and share these treasures of Afaan Oromoo with the world. Whether you are here to learn, to remember, or to find inspiration, I hope you find something that resonates with you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO ON THIS SITE

Discover — Browse through over 3,000 proverbs organized into more than 126 sections.

Search — Look for proverbs on specific themes like Jaalala (Love) or Gootummaa (Bravery).

Share — Use the Copy and Share buttons to spread the wisdom with your friends and family.

This project is a labor of love, born from a deep respect for our heritage. I invite you to explore, reflect, and share in the wisdom of our elders. Let's keep our language and culture alive, one proverb at a time.

Jabaadhu! (Be strong!)

— The Khoobaa Dubbii Team
